This documentation is an introductory guide to using Mr Robøt. It is intended to help you with the bot various controls and functions.

## What is Mr. Robøt ?

[Mr. Robøt](https://mrrobot.app) is a **Discord bot** which allows you to manage your Discord server. It allows you to delete insults from any member by default, but is configurable.

## 💥 **No bad word**

We've had enough of bad word too, which is why by default Mr Robøt protects you in any text channel.
Our basic filter automatically removes insults. So you don't have to worry about people wanting to damage your discussions.
A default list of bad words is displayed in the FAQ.

## 📜 **Logs**

Mr. Robøt allows you to view deleted messages, member arrivals and leave, invitation creations and much more.
You can also see messages containing bad words. You can configure the logs as you wish, it's up to you.

## 🔨 **Configurable**

Make your server the place you want it to be, adjust Mr. Robøt's settings to give more freedom or, on the contrary, more security for certain roles.
You can accept that certain roles may send bad words for various reasons.
The `/parameters` command is here for you!

## 🔎 **Utility commands**

Get information about the Discord server and even a certain user. Easier to ban and expel.
Spend more time chatting than moderating, check certain information on a Discord account. That's the magic of bots.
You have a multitude of controls at your disposal, and they will continue to grow and evolve over time.


:::note
To obtain all the commands, do **/help**.
All commands are posted on the website, [commands section](https://mrrobot.app/#commands).
:::

## Why is he called Mr. Robøt?

The robot refers to the series Mr. Robot, an American series created by Sam Esmail and broadcast since 24 June 2015 on USA Network.
We can therefore notice that the robot's face is in fact, the main character: Elliot Alderson.
